Android instructions

  1. Open this page in Chrome on an Android phone with NFC enabled.
  2. Enter a complete URL and tap Write to NFC tag.
  3. Hold the tag near the back of the phone until the page confirms the write.

If writing from this page doesn’t work, use NFC Tools on Android.

Get NFC Tools ↗

iPhone setup

Set up your tag in NFC Tools. iPhone browsers cannot write NFC tags.

  1. Download NFC Tools. Get NFC Tools from the App Store ↗
  2. Open NFC Tools and tap Write.
  3. Tap Add a record.
  4. For LinkedIn, choose Social Network, then LinkedIn. For another link, choose URL / Web link. Enter your destination directly in the app and save the record.
  5. Tap Write, then hold the tag near the top of your iPhone when the app asks you to scan it. Keep it there until the app confirms the write.
